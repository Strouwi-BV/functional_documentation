## Generating invoices
![New Invoice Button Highlighted](InvoicesOverviewNewInvoiceButtonHighlighted.png)
On the invoices overview, a new invoice can be generated by clicking the "new invoice" button. This will go to the invoice generation page.

### Without Client and Project Management

![Invoice Edit Buttons Highlighted](Invoice_edit_noCpm_clientEntered.png)
Client details like name, VAT-number and address have to be filled in manually.

![Invoice Edit Buttons Highlighted](Invoice_edit_noCpm_buttons.png)

1. Remove an invoiceline with the minus button next to a line
2. Add an invoice line by pressing on the left part of the large button
3. Generate the invoice by pressing "Genereer"

### With Client and Project Management
![Invoice Edit Select Client](Invoice_edit_selectClient.png)
When you select a [client](#clients), the clients name, VAT-number and address will automatically be added to the invoice.

![alt text](Invoice_edit_selectInvoiceLineType.png)
When you have the client and project management module, it is possible to select which type of invoiceline you want to add by pressing the arrow on the right of the button.

- "Voeg lijn toe"
    - ![Invoice Edit InvoiceLine](Invoice_edit_invoiceLine.png)
    - Always available
    - Has a description, VAT-percentage, price per item and an itemcount
    - Total is automatically calculated
- "Voeg werkuren toe"
    - ![Invoice Edit InvoiceLine Hours](Invoice_edit_invoiceLineHours.png)
    - Only available with client and project management module
    - Has an [employee](#user-details), [client](#clients), [project](#projects) VAT-percentage and worked hours
        - [Worked hours are calculated](#consult-employee-hours) based on the given timespan, employee, client and project.
        - Worked hours can be overwritten
            - ![Invoice Edit OverwriteHours](Invoice_edit_overwriteHours.png)
            - Pressing the red arrow will reset the value
    - Total is automatically calculated

### Pdf generation
![Invoice Edit Generated PDF](Invoice_edit_generatedPDF.png)
After clicking generate, a pdf will be shown. The PDF has the organizations logo, the organizations address, the clients address, the invoice details, a table containing the costs. There is also a request to make the payment to the organizations bankaccount and a hint to contact the organizations email in case the client has any questions.

![Invoice Edit PDF Actions](Invoice_edit_pdfActions.png)
You can zoom in on or download the PDF.

## Credit notes
When an invoice has the open or paid status, a credit note can be generated for it. This will copy the details and invert the costs to balance out the original invoice.

![Invoice Credit Note Button](Invoices_credit_note_button.png)
To create the credit note, go to the details page of an invoice, open the actions menu and click on the credit note button. This open a create page for the credit note.

![Invoice credit note create page](Invoice_credit_note_create_page.png)
The details and invoice lines are automatically added. Currently, the only other thing that is still required is the invoice number. Pressing generate will create the credit note.

![Invoice credit note reference](Invoice_credit_note_reference.png)
After a credit note is created, references will be made between the credit note and the original invoice. You can find them under the actions menu. Clicking on them will direct you to the reference detail page.
