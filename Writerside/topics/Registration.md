# Registration



### Login page

Here you can log in with the Email and password linked to your account. If your boss didn't give you a password check your mail inbox for a mail to setup a password or ask your boss what password he set up for your account.

If you are the owner of the company and want to register your company you can press the registration button at the bottom and follow the steps listed in "Trial registration" or "Paid registration".

![Login-page](MSS_login-page.jpg)

### Trial registration

At the login page press "registreer" to register a new company. This will lead you to the account information page. 
Fill in the information and choose the option "Gratis proefversie" for a 30 days free trial.

After reading the terms of conditions you accept them and press the "Start 30 dagen gratis" button, 
this will lead you to the dashboard where you can check out and test all the functions of the application.

![Trial-registration](MSS_trial-registration.jpg)





### Paid registration

At the login page press "registreer" to register a new company. This will lead you to the account information page.
Fill in the information and choose the option "Abonnement" to start a subscription.

After reading the terms of conditions you accept them and press the "Volgende" button,
this will lead you to the next step.

The next step is to add the company address, fill in the full company address and click on "volgende" to continue.
If you want to go back you can press "vorige".

When you continued you can now see what is included in the standard package, you here have to specify the amount of users
u want (u can always add more at any moment) and for how long you want to subscribe.

After filling in this info you will see the total cost, and you can press "volgende" to continue and "vorige" to go back.

This will lead you to the last step, this is where you select which payment method you want to use. This will bring you to the payment page of you chosen payment method.
After the payment is successfully done you will see the page where it says your payment succeeded and you can choose to go to the home page or to the settings.

If you payment did not go successful you will get to see a screen where it says the payment was cancelled and asks if you want to try again with a "klik hier" to go back to the registration page and try again to register the company.
